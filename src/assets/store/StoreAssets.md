## Full Description (both stores):

ParkNow solves one of the most frustrating daily problems — wasting 20 minutes circling markets looking for parking.

With ParkNow, drivers can:
- See real-time parking availability on a live map before they even leave home
- Reserve their exact spot in advance and pay securely in-app
- Navigate directly to their reserved spot
- Report free spots and earn ParkCoins rewards
- Use ParkCoins for discounts on future bookings

For parking lot owners:
- Monitor every spot live from a dashboard
- Scan QR codes to verify driver entry
- Track revenue with detailed reports
- Manage pricing and availability instantly

WHAT MAKES PARKNOW DIFFERENT:
Unlike general navigation apps, ParkNow is built specifically for market and mall parking with:
✓ Spot-level booking (not just lot-level)
✓ Community reporting like Waze for parking
✓ ParkCoins gamification for daily engagement
✓ Admin tools for lot owners

Built by Sudharshan Chowdary as a full-stack mobile project solving a validated problem with itch score 81 in Consumer Services.
